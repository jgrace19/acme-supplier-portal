About Acme Mutual Insurance Company

Protecting families and businesses since 1921. Policyholder-owned, headquartered in Hartford, Connecticut.

Who we are

Acme Mutual is a policyholder-owned property and casualty insurer writing personal auto, commercial auto and homeowners coverage in 18 states. We serve customers through approximately 3,400 independent agents and a growing direct channel, and we employ about 6,800 people, including approximately 2,100 in Claims across Hartford, Charlotte and Phoenix.

Strategy: Acme 2028

Our Acme 2028 plan focuses on three priorities:

  • Claims excellence. Faster, simpler claims through modern, cloud-based claims technology, with a goal of reducing cycle time for personal lines claims by 20%.
  • Catastrophe resilience. Scaling claims capacity quickly when severe weather strikes.
  • Trusted partners. Fewer, more strategic technology partners, held to clear service levels and strong security and privacy standards.
